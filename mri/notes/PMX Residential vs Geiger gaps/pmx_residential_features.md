# MRI PMX Residential Management: feature-level inventory (as of November 2026)

Research date: 2026-11-04.

**Scope.** This inventory covers the US residential configuration of MRI Property Management X (PMX), referred to below as "RM" (Residential Management). It includes:
- PMX Financials.
- The standard residential add-ons: Facility Management, Lockbox, DHCR, Affordable Housing hooks, Budgeting & Forecasting, Job Cost and others.
- The MRI Engage front-office suite and MRI-owned point products that plug into PMX RM.
- Partner-supplied capabilities, where MRI relies on partners for them.

**Purpose.** A line-by-line comparison against Geiger Property, using the 10-module taxonomy agreed for both inventories. Geiger was not researched for this file.

**Builds on** `mri/notes/MRI Software product portfolio/mri_residential_products.md`, `mri_corporate_and_portfolio_map.md`, `research_notes/Geiger vs MRI PMX fact check/mri_accounting_commercial.md`, `mri_platform_ai.md`, and `mri/reports/MRI Software product portfolio.md`. Those files cover product history, acquisitions and corporate context; this file adds feature granularity and does not repeat that context.

## How to read this file

- **IDs.** Each row has a stable ID, `PMX-<module>.<nn>`, for cross-referencing against the Geiger inventory. Some rows point to other rows ("see PMX-x.yy") so that each module stands on its own.
- **Licensing column:**
  - **Core RM**: part of PMX Residential Management as far as public sources show.
  - **Core Financials**: part of the PMX GL/AP base.
  - **Core platform / Core PMX**: platform features shipped with PMX.
  - **Add-on: \<name\>**: separately licensed or sold. MRI's functional spec says "Each module listed shall apply to the client if, and only if, a license ... is purchased" ([Functional spec](https://www.mrisoftware.com/mrifunctionalspecs/)).
  - **Partner-supplied: \<name\>**: delivered by an MRI Partner Connect partner.
  - **Unclear**: sources do not say.
  - MRI publishes no US price list or base-package contents, so many "add-on" tags rest on separate product pages, spec entries, status-page components or G-Cloud module lists.
- **Source flags.** "(snippet)" means search-result text only. "(inferred)" means deduced, not stated. "(partner)" or "(third party)" means not an MRI source. Everything else was fetched on 2026-11-04. Most sources are MRI marketing or training material, so treat capability claims as vendor statements.
- **Status labels.** **LEGACY** (MRI says existing clients only, or the product is being replaced), **REGIONAL** (non-US), **LIMITED AVAILABILITY**, **DEMO** and **NEW** (2025–26).
- **Unconfirmed.** Each module ends with an "Unconfirmed" list. These are capabilities common in US peers (Yardi, RealPage, Entrata, AppFolio, ResMan) that could not be confirmed for PMX Residential. Granular configuration detail (late-fee options, proration methods, NSF rules) sits behind MRI's client login (myMRI and in-product help). An "Unconfirmed" item may therefore exist without being publicly documented. Read it as "not evidenced", not as "absent".

## Product boundary and 2025–26 renames (read before using the licensing column)

MRI renamed its US multifamily front office from **MRI Living** to **MRI Engage** with "MRI Engage for Living" (21 Oct 2025). Engage is **not bundled** with PMX RM. ### Unconfirmed (module 1)

No public MRI source names these for PMX Residential, although peers commonly have them:
- A unit square-footage field
- Unit-level amenity premiums (amenity-based rent pricing)
- A floor-plan object separate from unit type
- Unit photos or marketing attributes stored in PMX (Market Connect held "photos and floor plan images" outside PMX)
- Geocoding of residential buildings (X.7.17 added coordinates for **CM** buildings only)
- A bulk property import for RM (a spreadsheet property import is documented only for Affordable Housing)

## Cross-cutting evidence gaps

- **MRI's detailed product documentation is behind login.** This includes in-product help, more than 2,500 myMRI knowledge articles, X.7 release notes and the "Product Ideas" voting portal. No public WebHelp instance was found; review sites (Capterra, G2, TrustRadius, GetApp) blocked automated fetches. Public evidence therefore stops at course-objective and release-highlight level. That is enough to prove a feature exists, but not how it is configured: late-fee options, proration rules, NSF fees, deposit-interest rules and approval routing are all undocumented publicly.
- **Licensing is rarely explicit.** No US price list or base-package definition is public. The only published PMX price is the UK G-Cloud entry bundle: £55,000/yr for Commercial Management, GL, AP and the Application Toolkit, with 500 leases and 10 users ([G-Cloud 15 PMX pricing](https://assets.applytosupply.digitalmarketplace.service.gov.uk/g-cloud-15/documents/709203/819137190684630-pricing-document-2026-01-29-0859.pdf)). Whether Facility Management, Lockbox, Report Gateway, Workflow Design or Application Studio authoring come in a typical US RM contract is unknown.
- **Two product generations coexist.** For example, Prospect Connect and Engage Apply, or Resident Connect and Engage Connect. Some rows describe legacy products that individual clients may still run (labelled LEGACY). The X.5 and X.7 clouds also run in parallel ([status page](https://status.mrisoftware.com/)).
- **Dating.** The course catalog is from Jan 2022 and the X.5 notes cover roughly 2019–2022. X.7.x items (2024–26) are one-paragraph summaries only. App-store version notes, the 2025–26 press releases and the 3 Nov 2026 release are the freshest evidence.
- **AI items are vendor marketing.** Several were "limited availability" (Jun 2025) or demos (2026). GA status is stated only for Agora Intelligence and Orchestrator (Jun 2026), Engage AI replies (Oct 2025) and the inbound voice agent. No independent outcome data exists.
- **Partner directory currency.** Some directory cards have no type or tier (for example Rainmaker LRO, Notifii, Esusu) and may be stale listings. Current integration status could not be verified without a Partner Connect login.
- **Regional content.** Where the only evidence is UK, international or affordable-housing material (step-ups, proration, service charges, waitlists, make-ready schedules), the row is labelled REGIONAL or Affordable, and US market-rate availability is listed as Unconfirmed.
