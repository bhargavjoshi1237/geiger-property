## 1. Corporate profile: HQ, founding, ownership, scale, leadership

### Takeaway
MRI is a private, PE-owned real estate software company headquartered in Solon, Ohio (Cleveland area). It was founded in 1971 as Management Reports Incorporated. It has been owned by a TA Associates / GI Partners / Harvest Partners consortium since January 2020, with TA the largest holder. In September 2025 the owners hired Goldman Sachs to explore a sale or IPO at up to about $10B including debt. As of 2026-11-03 I found **no announced sale, IPO filing, or recapitalization**. Estimated revenue is about $1B with about $400M EBITDA. MRI claims 45,000+ clients in 170+ countries and 6M+ users. Patrick Ghilani is still CEO. 2026 brought a new CRO, a new Chief Client Officer and a Chief Innovation Officer.

### Inferences
- The jump from about 8,500 "enterprise clients" (2020) to 45,000+ "clients" (2026) most likely reflects acquired SMB-heavy books: RentPayment payers, ANZ agencies on Property Tree/Palace/PTG, UK councils and housing associations, and screening customers. The two figures should not be read as like-for-like growth.
- "Nearly 5,000 employees" fits the deal count and about $1B revenue, but no MRI-primary source confirms it.
- The 12-month window Reuters gave for a deal expired around September 2026 with no announced transaction. Ownership as of November 2026 should therefore be described as "TA Associates (largest), GI Partners, Harvest Partners; exit exploration reported 2025, no deal announced."

- In practice, "MRI Commercial Management" and "MRI Residential Management" are the commercial and residential configurations of one MRI property and financial database, historically "MRI Platform X," now marketed as PMX. ### Gaps

- Deployment (SaaS-only vs hosted vs on-prem) is not stated on the PMX page.

## 6. Sales and pricing model

### Takeaway
MRI sells through enterprise, quote-based contracts with separately billed implementation. It publishes no general price list. The only official public price points found are MRI's UK G-Cloud 15 filings (January 2026). Those show **unit-tiered** pricing for a communications module and **lease-count-tiered** pricing for lease management, with day-rate implementation. Typical customers range from mid-market to the largest enterprises. The ANZ, payments and screening lines reach SMBs.

### Cited Findings
- G-Cloud 15 UK (January 2026): Communications Manager costs £17,000 for up to 2,000 managed units, rising to £32,500 for up to 30,000 units, plus £1 per additional unit. Lease management starts at £9,995/year for up to 25 leases. Implementation is billed at £162/hour (project management) and £200/hour (specialist technical). Prices exclude VAT (snippet summary of MRI pricing PDFs). — [G-Cloud 15 pricing doc](https://assets.applytosupply.digitalmarketplace.service.gov.uk/g-cloud-15/documents/709203/531914968047280-pricing-document-2026-01-28-1620.pdf); [second doc](https://assets.applytosupply.digitalmarketplace.service.gov.uk/g-cloud-15/documents/709203/479870099738632-pricing-document-2026-01-30-0944.pdf)
- Third-party pricing pages describe MRI as quote-based, with portfolio size and modules as the main drivers. Third-party per-unit or "$55/month" figures are inconsistent and not credible. — [Facilio](https://facilio.com/blog/mri-software-pricing/); [ERP Research](https://erpresearch.com/erp-add-ons/real-estate/mri-software/pricing)
- Implementation channels: MRI Professional and Managed Services, plus certified partners. Loci Solutions was "an MRI Certified Partner specialising in the implementation of property management systems" before MRI bought it in 2023. ### Inferences
- Pricing is likely per unit (residential), per lease or per square foot (commercial and occupier), and per user (some modules), set by quote. The G-Cloud tiers confirm unit and lease metrics in at least the UK. I found no public per-square-foot evidence.
- PMX (about 2,500 clients) is the enterprise core. Most of the 45,000+ "clients" sit on regional, SMB or transactional products.

### Gaps
- No US list prices, typical ACV, or implementation duration and cost from a credible source.
- I did not open the G-Cloud PDFs directly; the figures come from a search summary.
