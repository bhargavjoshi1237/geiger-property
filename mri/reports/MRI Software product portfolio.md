# MRI's sprawling portfolio skips Geiger's buyers

MRI Software is a private-equity-owned real estate software company based in Ohio, with revenue of roughly $1 billion. About 55 acquisitions have given it more than a hundred products. They span US multifamily, affordable and public housing, commercial and retail leasing, corporate lease accounting, workplace and facilities management, investment modelling, UK social housing and council systems, and letting agencies in Australia and New Zealand. MRI even owns a shipping-container software unit. Its Agora data and AI layer is now stretched across all of it. For Geiger Property, the MRI product that matters most is **PMX Residential Management, together with the MRI Engage front office and RentPayment**. It does the same job Geiger does. But MRI sells it to mid-size and large operators on quote-based contracts, with rollouts that take months, and **MRI has no self-serve or small-landlord product in the US**. Geiger's closest product twin is **MRI Property Tree** (and its New Zealand sibling, Palace). Property Tree is a small-agency system with published per-property pricing, but it sells only in Australia and New Zealand, so it is a benchmark rather than a rival. MRI is therefore Geiger's upmarket ceiling, not its day-one competitor; AppFolio, Buildium, Rent Manager and Yardi Breeze fill that role. MRI's documented weaknesses line up closely with what Geiger plans to offer. They are opaque pricing with paid extras, consultant-led rollouts, below-par usability scores, a 1.5-star payments app, gated APIs, and support strain while a sale is pending. Yet Geiger can claim few of these advantages today. It has not launched, its data is not secured, and screening, e-signature, SMS, a public API and AI are all missing or tracking-only. Geiger should stay out of the lines where MRI is entrenched and Geiger has nothing: occupier lease accounting, affordable and public housing, the UK public sector, investment management, workplace and facilities, enterprise commercial and retail, and the Australia and New Zealand agency market.

## A $1 billion acquisition engine is still waiting for its buyer

MRI began in Cleveland in 1971 as Management Reports Incorporated. Intuit bought it in 2002, and Vista Equity Partners bought it in 2009-10 ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MRI_Software)). Private equity has owned it ever since: GI Partners bought it in 2015, TA Associates invested in 2017, and Harvest Partners joined in January 2020, when the three firms took equal board representation ([MRI](https://www.mrisoftware.com/news/mri-software-receives-investment-accelerate-innovation-global-growth/)). Its headquarters is in Solon, Ohio ([MRI About](https://www.mrisoftware.com/about/)). In September 2025 Reuters reported that the owners had hired Goldman Sachs to explore **a sale or US listing at up to $10 billion including debt**. According to Reuters, revenue was "approaching $1 billion", with about **$400 million of EBITDA**, roughly 10% annual growth, and more than half of revenue from outside the US ([Reuters via Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/exclusive-private-equity-owners-mri-173131792.html)). The 12-month window Reuters described has passed without an announced deal. MRI's 2026 newsroom has awards, AI launches and executive hires (a new chief revenue officer in May, a chief client officer in August), but no ownership news ([MRI News](https://www.mrisoftware.com/news/)). As of November 2026, the accurate description is: owned by TA Associates (the largest holder), GI Partners and Harvest Partners, with an exit explored but not carried out.

MRI's headline scale needs careful reading. It claims **45,000+ clients, 16.5 million units, 5.5 million leases and 170+ countries** ([MRI About](https://www.mrisoftware.com/about/)), up from "more than 8,500 enterprise clients" in January 2020 ([MRI](https://www.mrisoftware.com/news/mri-software-receives-investment-accelerate-innovation-global-growth/)). Most of that jump came from buying books of small customers, such as Australian and New Zealand agencies, UK councils, and payment and screening users, rather than from enterprise growth. The flagship PMX platform reported just **2,500+ clients and 50,000 users** when it was relaunched in 2024 ([MRI PMX release](https://www.mrisoftware.com/news/revolutionizing-real-estate-efficiency-next-generation-property-management-system/)). In other words, most MRI customers do not use the product most people mean by "MRI". MRI says it added nearly 2,000 new clients in 2025, about 20% more than the year before. That is a vendor claim ([News on the Block](https://webhook.newsontheblock.com/news-opinion/mri-software-reports-record-year-after-adding-nearly-2-000-new-clients-and-a-surge-in-ai-platform-adoption)).

The portfolio came from roughly **55 acquisitions since 2011**. Tracxn counts 53 ([Tracxn](https://tracxn.com/d/acquisitions/acquisitions-by-mri-software/__g6peePXS7eu_VZPHGhwFBU72HWDr-CXa-DPzn8qVVYo)), and MRI's own sitemap lists about 52 unique acquisition releases ([MRI sitemap](https://www.mrisoftware.com/sitemap/)). The deals follow clear themes:

- **US public and affordable housing roll-up (2017-2020):** Tenmast, HAB, HAPPY, IPM and Lindsey.
- **UK expansion, then a UK social housing roll-up:** Qube, Orchard, Castleton and Housing Partners, capped in 2024 by Capita One's council software ([MRI](https://www.mrisoftware.com/news/mri-software-completes-acquisition-of-capita-one-to-expand-uk-public-sector-offering/)).
- **Australia and New Zealand residential consolidation:** Rockend, Palace, PropTech Group and Proptech Labs.
- **An occupier lease stack:** LEVERTON, ProLease, MacMunnis, AMTdirect and LeaseEagle.
- **Facilities, energy and workplace:** Manhattan, WhosOnLocation, FSI, eSight, Angus and Springboard.

Smaller payments, screening and insurance deals ran throughout. The most recent confirmed deals were Anacle of Singapore in June 2025 ([MRI](https://www.mrisoftware.com/news/mri-software-completes-acquisition-of-anacle-a-leading-singapore-based-provider-of-property-and-facilities-management-solutions/)) and Proptech Labs of Melbourne in October 2025 ([MRI](https://www.mrisoftware.com/news/mri-software-acquires-proptech-labs-to-deliver-all-in-one-property-management-solutions-across-australia-new-zealand/)). **No acquisition has been announced in 2026.** That fits an owner preparing an exit and relying on in-house AI products. MRI also runs businesses unrelated to property management. One is MRI Intermodal, which sells container-leasing and depot software and grew with the purchase of India's iInterchange in December 2024 ([MRI](https://www.mrisoftware.com/news/mri-software-acquires-iinterchange-to-expand-global-intermodal-software-business/)).

MRI does not organise itself by the segments a competitor would use. Its site navigation groups products by function (Property Management & Financials; Facilities, Energy & Workplace; Marketing, Sales & Leasing; Risk & Fraud Management; Lease Management), and separately by sector ([MRI](https://www.mrisoftware.com/)). Each regional site also publishes its own A-Z, with products sold only in that region ([MRI UK A-Z](https://www.mrisoftware.com/uk/products/); [MRI AU A-Z](https://www.mrisoftware.com/au/products/)). ### Housing: an enterprise multifamily stack, a subsidised-housing franchise and regional agency systems

MRI's US multifamily offer has two layers. The back office is **PMX Residential Management**, which MRI describes as handling "accounting, operations and the ledger the front office writes to" ([MRI multifamily](https://mrisoftware.com/living/)). The front office is **MRI Engage**, a 2024-2025 rebuild of the old "MRI Living" brand. Engage CRM launched in June 2024 ([Mann Report](https://www.mannpublications.com/mannreport/2024/06/18/mri-software-introduces-unified-multifamily-crm-and-communications-tool/)). The Engage for Living suite followed on 21 October 2025. It added resident and leasing portals, embedded payments, fraud tools, e-signatures, and AI-suggested replies that staff can choose to send automatically ([Mann Report](https://www.mannpublications.com/mannreport/2025/10/21/mri-software-delivers-all-in-one-multifamily-engagement-suite/)). The CRM also includes a 24/7 AI voice leasing agent, syncs guest cards to PMX in real time, and typically takes **60-90 days to implement** ([MRI Multifamily CRM](https://www.mrisoftware.com/multifamily/multifamily-crm/)). Around these two layers, MRI owns tools for:

- screening (ResidentCheck)
- ID verification (CheckpointID) ([MRI](https://www.mrisoftware.com/news/mri-software-acquires-checkpointid-adding-id-verification-and-fraud-prevention-to-multifamily-offering/))
- payments (RentPayment)
- renters insurance
- lease guarantees (Insurent) ([MRI](https://www.mrisoftware.com/news/mri-software-acquires-insurent-guarantor-insurance-services/))
- market data (ApartmentData) ([PR Newswire](https://prnewswire.com/news-releases/mri-software-acquires-apartmentdatacom-houston-based-provider-of-market-research-and-data-solutions-for-multifamily-properties-301599899.html))

Revenue management and utility billing are probably supplied by partners: the research found no MRI-built product for either.

Two signals stand out. First, MRI sells Engage to **Yardi Voyager** customers as well as to PMX clients ([MRI](https://www.mrisoftware.com/news/mri-software-delivers-all-in-one-multifamily-engagement-suite/)). It now competes for front-office spending regardless of which back-office system a customer runs. Second, MRI's claim that "88%+" of the NMHC Top 50 managers use MRI ([MRI multifamily](https://mrisoftware.com/living/)) counts any MRI product, not just the main operating system. MRI pitches PMX to single-family rental operators, but offers no small-landlord tier ([MRI SFR](https://www.mrisoftware.com/single-family-property-management-software/)). Its residential hub lists multifamily, affordable, public, condo/HOA and single-family housing, but **not student, senior or military housing** ([MRI residential hub](https://mrisoftware.com/residential)). Homeowner and condo associations get Jenark, bought from CoreLogic in 2020 with about half a million units, and CondoPlus ([MRI](https://www.mrisoftware.com/news/mri-software-acquires-jenark-from-corelogic); [MRI Condo/HOA](https://www.mrisoftware.com/solutions/condo-hoa-management-software/)).

Subsidised housing is where the acquisition strategy built a real franchise. **MRI Affordable Housing** is a compliance module on PMX. It covers HUD multifamily, tax credit, HOME and USDA Rural Development programs; sends rent-subsidy (HAP) files to HUD's TRACS system; and runs tenant certifications, waitlists and subsidy accounting ([MRI Affordable Housing](https://www.mrisoftware.com/products/affordable-housing-compliance-software/)). It requires Residential Management, the General Ledger, Accounts Payable and other modules ([MRI Functional Specifications](https://www.mrisoftware.com/mrifunctionalspecs/)), which makes it an enterprise purchase. MRI claims more than 500 affordable owners and operators. On the public housing side, MRI bought Tenmast and HAB in 2017, HAPPY Software in 2017 or 2018 (sources disagree), and Lindsey Software, used by more than 700 housing authorities, in 2020 ([MRI](https://www.mrisoftware.com/news/mri-software-acquires-lindsey-software-public-housing-solutions/)). In May 2024 it launched **PHA Pro** as its "flagship public housing and voucher management compliance solution" ([MRI](https://mrisoftware.com/news/mri-software-unveils-pha-pro-integrated-public-housing-voucher-management-compliance)). The current product page claims 1,260+ public housing clients ([MRI Public Housing](https://www.mrisoftware.com/products/public-housing-software/)), while the May 2024 release said more than 1,600. This franchise also accounts for most of MRI's Trustpilot reviews: the MRI_HAB product page alone holds about 3,400 of them ([Trustpilot](https://ca.trustpilot.com/review/www.mrisoftware.com/product/mrihab-649f4e12c13a8cecdf7a1daf)).

Outside the US, MRI's residential business looks nothing like PMX.

- **Australia and New Zealand.** MRI runs letting-agency software here:
  - **Property Tree**, and the older desktop product **Rest Professional**, both from Rockend (2019).
  - **Strata Master**, for strata and body corporate management.
  - **Palace** in New Zealand, which had 700+ clients and 150,000+ properties when MRI bought it in 2021 ([MRI](https://www.mrisoftware.com/news/mri-software-acquires-palace-new-zealand-property-management-provider)).
  - The VaultRE agency CRM, from the A$93.4 million PropTech Group takeover in 2023 ([MRI](https://www.mrisoftware.com/news/mri-software-completes-acquisition-proptech-group-asx-ptg/)).
  - Maintenance, inspection, key-management and AI chatbot tools from Proptech Labs (October 2025). Its Key Automate tool was folded into Property Tree and Palace subscriptions ([MRI AU](https://www.mrisoftware.com/au/news/mri-software-acquires-proptech-labs-to-deliver-all-in-one-property-management-solutions-across-australia-new-zealand)).
- **Property Tree pricing.** Property Tree is MRI's only property-management product with a **published price**: A$130, A$160 or A$205 per 100 properties per month, excluding GST. Every tier includes unlimited users, owner and tenant portals, trust accounting, maintenance, inspections and AI invoice processing, and MRI promises setup "within 48 hours" ([MRI Property Tree pricing](https://www.mrisoftware.com/au/products/property-tree/pricing-1/)). A consultancy recap of MRI's 2026 Asia-Pacific conference reports 10,000 clients in the region ([REdirect Consulting](https://www.redirectconsulting.com/blog/mri-ascend-apac-2026-mri-agora-ai-agentic-automation-and-the-future-of-pmx)).
- **UK and Ireland.** MRI sells Qube Property Management for block management and build-to-rent, estate-agency software from CML and Thesaurus, and the Engage resident portal. JAR, a London block manager, became the first publicly named residential customer of Agora Orchestrator in October 2026 ([BeBeez](https://bebeez.eu/2026/10/06/jar-adopts-mri-softwares-agentic-ai-to-streamline-workflows-and-support-resident-service/)).
- **UK social housing and local government.** This is a separate business, built from:
  - Orchard ([PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/mri-software-broadens-capabilities-with-orchard-information-systems-acquisition-301032413.html))
  - Castleton, taken private in 2020 ([MRI](https://www.mrisoftware.com/news/mri-software-completes-acquisition-castleton-expand-social-housing-uk/))
  - Housing Partners ([MRI](https://www.mrisoftware.com/news/mri-software-acquires-housing-partners-to-broaden-front-line-digital-services-for-social-housing-tenants/))
  - Capita One's council software for revenues, benefits and education
- **South Africa.** MRI owns the MDA commercial system, used by about 70% of listed South African REITs ([MRI ZA](https://www.mrisoftware.com/za/news/mri-software-acquires-mda-property-systems-leader-real-estate-software-african-market/)), and the TPN tenant credit bureau ([MRI](https://www.mrisoftware.com/news/mri-software-announces-acquisition-of-tpn-group-south-africas-leading-specialist-credit-bureau/)).

### Commercial, occupier, workplace and investment: separate buyers, mostly separate code

For commercial landlords, MRI's engine is **PMX Commercial Management**. It handles rent, pools of shared expenses, tenant recovery profiles and year-end reconciliations ([MRI blog](https://www.mrisoftware.com/ie/?p=25458)). The **Advanced Retail** add-on handles percentage rent, sales thresholds (breakpoints) and sales tracking ([MRI Advanced Retail](https://www.mrisoftware.com/products/advanced-retail/)). Tenants report their sales through the Tenant Connect portal rather than through automatic point-of-sale feeds. The other main pieces are:

- **Lease Flow**, the leasing CRM and deal-modelling tool, which integrates directly with Commercial Management ([MRI Lease Flow](https://www.mrisoftware.com/products/leaseflow/)).
- **Contract Intelligence**, formerly LEVERTON, which extracts lease data in 25+ languages, with layers of human review ([MRI Contract Intelligence](https://www.mrisoftware.com/products/contract-intelligence/)).
- **Engage @Work** (2023), the commercial tenant app ([MRI](https://www.mrisoftware.com/news/mri-software-launches-flexible-property-portal-to-drive-tenant-engagement/)).
- **MRI Angus**, which runs building operations, tenant requests and insurance-certificate tracking. MRI bought it with 700+ customers and 2.7 billion square feet under management ([Commercial Observer](https://commercialobserver.com/2022/01/mri-software-angus-systems); [MRI Angus](https://www.mrisoftware.com/products/angus/)).

Since June 2025 MRI has also owned a second commercial engine, **MRI Simplicity**, which came with Anacle and is aimed at portfolios in Asia ([MRI UK](https://www.mrisoftware.com/uk/?p=85683); [MRI AU](https://www.mrisoftware.com/au/news/anacle-systems-mri-software-rebrand/)). Accounts-payable automation comes from partners: Nexus outside the US (owned by Bottomline since 2022) and AvidXchange inside it ([Bottomline](https://bottomline.com/newsroom/press-releases/bottomline-acquires-nexus-systems); [MRI CA](https://www.mrisoftware.com/ca/?p=23216)).

The **occupier lease line**, which serves companies that rent their own space, is the strongest standalone business in the portfolio. **MRI ProLease** comes in three tiers: Enterprise (the former Qube Horizon, per a UK G-Cloud listing), Central and Direct. It covers real estate and equipment leases and handles the ASC 842, IFRS 16, GASB 87 and FRS 102 lease-accounting standards. MRI claims **3,500 occupier clients** ([MRI ProLease](https://www.mrisoftware.com/products/prolease/); [G-Cloud](https://www.applytosupply.digitalmarketplace.service.gov.uk/g-cloud-14/services/813712730500263)). In October 2026 MRI announced that IDC had named it a Leader in its MarketScape for AI-enabled lease administration, ranked highest on capabilities. That placement rests on MRI's release; the IDC report itself was not seen ([MRI](https://www.mrisoftware.com/news/mri-software-named-a-leader-in-2026-idc-marketscape-for-worldwide-ai-enabled-lease-administration-applications/)). AMTdirect, bought in 2021 ([MRI](https://www.mrisoftware.com/news/mri-software-acquires-amtdirect-lease-facilities-management)), no longer appears in the 2026 product index, which points to consolidation under the ProLease brand. ProLease has the best review scores of MRI's products, 4.4 on Capterra from 22 reviews ([Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/210809/MRI-ProLease/reviews/)).

**Workplace and facilities** products are almost all acquired. **ManhattanONE** is an integrated workplace management system (IWMS) bought from Trimble in 2021, along with more than 3,000 corporate occupier clients ([MRI](https://www.mrisoftware.com/news/mri-software-completes-acquisition-manhattan-real-estate-workplace-solutions/)). It has modules for space, room booking, projects, facilities maintenance and sustainability ([MRI ManhattanONE](https://www.mrisoftware.com/products/manhattanone/)). Verdantix named MRI one of eight leaders in its January 2025 IWMS Green Quadrant ([Verdantix](https://www.verdantix.com/report/real-estate-built-environment/green-quadrant-connected-portfolio-intelligence-platforms-cpip-iwms-2025)). Alongside it sit:

- Workplace Central, for space planning and booking ([MRI](https://mrisoftware.com/products/workplace-central))
- **MRI Evolution**, FSI's computer-aided facilities management system, with 300+ clients ([MRI SG](https://www.mrisoftware.com/sg/?p=40041))
- NETfacilities
- the OnLocation visitor and footfall products
- Asset4000 fixed-asset accounting, from RAM
- **MRI Energy**, formerly eSight, which one review site calls "not a full ESG platform" ([Net Zero Compare](https://netzerocompare.com/software/mri-software))

The result is at least four overlapping work-order products: Angus, Evolution, NETfacilities and ManhattanONE's maintenance module. None has been announced for retirement. MRI is also missing from Verdantix's 2025 list of leaders in maintenance management software (CMMS) ([Verdantix](https://www.verdantix.com/insights/press-release/verdantix-green-quadrant-report-highlights-leading-commercial-buildings-cmms-vendors-enabling-real-estate-optimization-and-resource-efficiency)).

**Investment management** is a separately licensed suite, not part of PMX. Its modelling layer comes from Cougar Software (2015). It includes Asset Modeling (capital projects and scenarios against a target return), Fund Modeling, Valuations and Debt Management ([MRI investment](https://www.mrisoftware.com/products/investment-modeling/); [MRI](https://mrisoftware.com/nz/news/mri-software-announces-acquisition-of-cougar-software)). Its accounting layer was built in-house in 2016 on MRI Financials. Investment Accounting adds capital calls, an engine for splitting distributions between investors (a waterfall) and consolidations ([MRI](https://www.mrisoftware.com/news/mri-software-announces-launch-investment-accounting/)). Investment Central handles asset management, including tax-credit portfolios, and Investor Connect handles investor reporting. MRI sells no loan-servicing software for lenders.

The portfolio therefore has two tiers of integration:

- **A shared-ledger tier** rolls property transactions into enterprise and fund accounting. It includes PMX Financials, Commercial and Residential Management, Advanced Retail, Investment Accounting, budgeting and job costing.
- **A standalone tier** sells to different buyers and connects only through individual integrations. It includes ProLease, ManhattanONE, Evolution, Angus, Energy, OnLocation, Valuations and Simplicity. The Angus and ManhattanONE pages describe no PMX integration at all ([MRI Angus](https://www.mrisoftware.com/products/angus/); [MRI ManhattanONE](https://www.mrisoftware.com/products/manhattanone/)).

The regional engines (Property Tree, Palace, Qube PM and the South African products) form a third, parallel layer.

## Agora is the glue, and PMX does have a low-code studio

MRI's answer to its own fragmentation is **MRI Agora**. It launched in October 2022 as an "open and connected", AI-first platform, with Agora Insights providing analytics across products ([MRI](https://www.mrisoftware.com/news/mri-software-unveils-its-next-generation-real-estate-platform/)). At the time, Verdantix described Agora as a data lake on Microsoft Azure plus shared services. It warned that full delivery "is not going to happen overnight", and noted that MRI's earlier acquisitions had produced little synergy, leaving customers of several MRI apps moving data by hand and in Excel ([Verdantix](https://www.verdantix.com/insights/blog/where-ambition-meets-innovation-mri-software-unveils-new-agora-platform)). Since then Agora has become MRI's AI story:

- **7 March 2024:** **Ask Agora** first appeared in the PMX release, as plain-language questions over the PMX database ([MRI PMX release](https://www.mrisoftware.com/news/revolutionizing-real-estate-efficiency-next-generation-property-management-system/)).
- **June 2025:** an AI agent for month-end close and an Ask Agora page assistant went into limited availability ([MRI](https://www.mrisoftware.com/news/mri-software-introduces-ai-agent-and-page-assistant-to-transform-property-management-operations/)).
- **20 October 2025:** Ask Agora was relaunched as an "AI companion" that works across products ([PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/mri-software-unveils-advanced-ai-companion-to-transform-real-estate-workflows-302588719.html)).
- **25 June 2026:** **Agora Intelligence** launched, sending role-based daily briefings. So did **Agora Orchestrator**, which runs AI agent workflows from a pre-built catalog, with escalation to humans and an audit trail ([MRI](https://www.mrisoftware.com/news/mri-software-launches-agora-intelligence-and-agora-orchestrator-delivering-ai-powered-recommendations-and-workflow-execution-for-real-estate/)).

Agora reached Europe, the Middle East and Africa in June 2026 and Asia-Pacific in August 2026 ([REdirect Consulting EMEA](https://www.redirectconsulting.com/blog/mri-emea-ascend-2026-how-agora-ai-and-agentic-automation-are-transforming-property-management); [REdirect Consulting APAC](https://www.redirectconsulting.com/blog/mri-ascend-apac-2026-mri-agora-ai-agentic-automation-and-the-future-of-pmx)). AI also sits inside individual products: Engage's suggested replies and voice agent, Property Tree's AI invoice processing and image recognition, and Contract Intelligence. MRI claims more than a quarter of a million monthly active users of its AI features ([MRI](https://www.mrisoftware.com/news/mri-software-unveils-advanced-ai-companion-to-transform-real-estate-workflows/)) and a 98% cut in the time needed to create finance reports ([News on the Block](https://webhook.newsontheblock.com/news-opinion/mri-software-reports-record-year-after-adding-nearly-2-000-new-clients-and-a-surge-in-ai-platform-adoption)). No independent outcome data exists, and the launch materials name no measured customer results.

One correction matters for any feature comparison. An internal fact-check dated 2 November 2026 found no MRI low-code builder. That verdict is too strong. MRI's own 7 March 2024 release describes **"PMX Studio Suite", a web-based toolkit for building tailored pages, reports and workflows** ([MRI PMX release](https://www.mrisoftware.com/news/revolutionizing-real-estate-efficiency-next-generation-property-management-system/)). Material from MRI's 2026 conferences presents PMX 10.7 plus an "Application Studio" as the upgrade path ([REdirect Consulting](https://www.redirectconsulting.com/blog/mri-ascend-apac-2026-mri-agora-ai-agentic-automation-and-the-future-of-pmx)). The current Agora page adds a **Workflow Builder**, which maps a client's technology and recommends what to automate ([MRI Agora](https://www.mrisoftware.com/mri-agora/)). What remains unproven is depth. No source shows clients defining their own database tables or writing their own Orchestrator agents. The fair verdict is that PMX offers configurable pages, reports and workflows, but not a Salesforce-style platform for building apps. For Geiger, this means its no-code workflow canvas at best matches PMX; it is not a differentiator.

MRI's "open and connected" claim holds for distribution, but not for developers. MRI counts 400+ partners and says the partner program grew 60% from 2022 to 2025 ([MRI](https://www.mrisoftware.com/news/mri-software-named-to-inc-5000-list-of-fastest-growing-private-companies-for-fifth-time/)). Its Application eXchange holds customisations shared by clients, with "over 3,400" downloads; it is not a store for third-party apps ([MRI MAX](https://www.mrisoftware.com/products/mri-application-exchange/)). API access is gated, with no self-service developer portal and no general testing sandbox ([Apideck](https://developers.apideck.com/apis/accounting/mrisoftware/configuration)). Propexo, an integration vendor, reports that partners pay **$5,000-$15,000 a year plus a 2.5-15% revenue share** to join Partner Connect, while clients pay about **$10,000 a year for MIX API access** ([Propexo](https://docs.propexo.com/pms-guidance/mri/integration-paths)). In the UK, API packs are priced add-ons ([G-Cloud 15 Evolution pricing](https://assets.applytosupply.digitalmarketplace.service.gov.uk/g-cloud-15/documents/709203/231765895062207-pricing-document-2026-01-29-0843.pdf)). So is Agora Insights, at 15% of annual contract value with an £8,000 minimum ([Facilio, a competitor, citing G-Cloud 14](https://facilio.com/blog/mri-software-pricing/)).

## Four contradictions in the notes resolve, with different confidence

| Question | Resolution | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| Does MRI own Bricks+Agent? | Probably, as part of Proptech Labs (October 2025). It was not a standalone MRI acquisition, and MRI has never named it. | **Moderate** that MRI owns it; **high** that there was no separate deal |
| Did Ask Agora launch in May 2024 or October 2025? | Neither date is the first. The name and first feature appeared in the US PMX release of 7 March 2024; May 2024 was the Asia-Pacific relaunch; October 2025 relaunched it as an AI companion across products. | **High** |
| Did MRI cut about 200 jobs in May 2026? | Yes, a reorganisation with job cuts took place. The figure of 200 has been reported but MRI has not confirmed it. | **High** for the cuts; **moderate** for the number; **low** for the breakdown by department |
| Are Residential Management and Commercial Management separate products? | No. They are licensed modules, or configurations, of PMX and share PMX Financials. They appear "separate" only as review-site listings and as separately priced modules. | **High** that the platform is shared; **moderate** on the exact naming history |

**Bricks+Agent.** The two notes agree on the facts and differ only in how they read them. No MRI release names Bricks+Agent, and it is not among MRI's acquisition announcements ([MRI sitemap](https://www.mrisoftware.com/sitemap/)). But the evidence chain points one way:

- Proptech Labs' own website lists Bricks+Agent as one of its products ([Proptech Labs](https://www.proptechlabs.com/)).
- Bricks+Agent was founded in 2016 as a maintenance and inspections platform serving Melbourne, Sydney and London ([Bricks+Agent](https://bricksandagent.com/about-us)).
- Its co-founder, Rafael Niesten ([Craft](https://craft.co/bricks-agent)), is the Proptech Labs chief executive quoted in MRI's October 2025 acquisition release. That release lists maintenance and inspections among the capabilities moving into Property Tree and Palace ([MRI AU](https://www.mrisoftware.com/au/news/mri-software-acquires-proptech-labs-to-deliver-all-in-one-property-management-solutions-across-australia-new-zealand)).

The most likely reading is that Bricks+Agent came to MRI inside Proptech Labs. Confidence stays moderate because MRI has never said so, and Bricks+Agent could be held by a separate company. Describe it internally as "probably MRI-owned through Proptech Labs" and do not state it as fact in external material. Whether its London operations continue is unknown.

**Ask Agora.** Both dates are real, but they describe different launches. The 7 March 2024 US PMX release introduced Ask Agora as plain-language questions over the PMX database ([MRI PMX release](https://www.mrisoftware.com/news/revolutionizing-real-estate-efficiency-next-generation-property-management-system/)). The 8 May 2024 coverage repeated that bundle for the Asia-Pacific launch ([Elite Agent](https://eliteagent.com/mri-software-announces-next-generation-property-management-tech/)). The 20 October 2025 release recast Ask Agora as an "AI companion" that uses AI agents and data from many sources ([PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/mri-software-unveils-advanced-ai-companion-to-transform-real-estate-workflows-302588719.html)). One note's entry, "May 2024: Agora platform and Ask Agora", also misdates Agora itself, which launched in October 2022. Confidence is high because both ends of the timeline come from MRI's own releases.

**May 2026 layoffs.** The corporate-profile researcher found only blog reports ([The AI Consulting Network](https://www.theaiconsultingnetwork.com/blog/mri-software-200-layoffs-10b-sale-ai-cre-investors-2026)). The market researcher found a Propmodo report of 11 May 2026, citing Crain's Cleveland Business. It said about 200 positions were cut in a "global workforce reorganization" tied to AI adoption. MRI said it expected to "improve scalability and responsiveness without a meaningful impact to overall headcount or client resources" and did not confirm the number ([Propmodo](https://propmodo.com/private-equity-backed-proptech-firm-cuts-200-jobs-cites-ai-adoption/)). A trade-press report plus a company statement settles that a reorganisation with job cuts happened. The figure of about 200 rests on one original outlet, whose article was not retrieved, and MRI has not confirmed it. The claim that engineering, sales and support were hit hardest comes only from a blog. MRI's "no meaningful impact to overall headcount" wording implies staff were redeployed or roles refilled. Glassdoor review titles mention "Mass layoffs and outsourcing" ([Glassdoor](https://www.glassdoor.com/Reviews/Employee-Review-MRI-Software-E325785-RVW98780878.htm)), but outsourcing is unproven.

**Residential and Commercial Management.** MRI's own pages treat both as parts of PMX:

- MRI's Ireland PMX page lists both as PMX modules ([MRI IE PMX](https://www.mrisoftware.com/ie/products/property-management-x/)).
- Its South African site calls them "the commercial-focused module of PMX" and "PMX's residential setup" ([MRI ZA](https://mrisoftware.com/za/?p=29037)).
- Its Engage pages refer to "PMX Residential" ([MRI Multifamily CRM](https://www.mrisoftware.com/multifamily/multifamily-crm/)).
- The PMX page covers residential, office, industrial, retail and mixed-use property on one general ledger ([MRI PMX](https://www.mrisoftware.com/products/property-management-x/)).

The impression of separate products comes from two places. Capterra and GetApp keep separate "MRI Residential Management" and "MRI Commercial Management" listings, each with its own ratings ([Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/226831/MRI-Residential-Management/reviews/)). And the names predate PMX: the same platform shipped as "Version X" from 2014 ([MRI](https://www.mrisoftware.com/news/mri-software-debuts-its-latest-innovative-solution-mri-version-x)). MRI publishes no official map from Platform X through Residential and Commercial Management to PMX, so the exact naming history is inferred. The practical consequence is that review scores for those listings are scores for PMX modules, probably on versions from before the 2024 redesign.

Two corrections to earlier work follow from the same evidence. First, **PMX was announced in a US release on 7 March 2024, not in May 2024**. May was the Asia-Pacific launch ([MRI AU](https://www.mrisoftware.com/au/news/revolutionising-real-estate-efficiency-next-generation-property-management-system/); [Elite Agent](https://eliteagent.com/mri-software-announces-next-generation-property-management-tech/)). Second, the Studio Suite evidence, covered in the previous section, changes the low-code verdict. Several smaller inconsistencies also resolve:

- **Engage for Living** launched on 21 October 2025, the date of MRI's release and the Mann Report article. A November 2025 article was later coverage.
- **Anacle's completion** is confirmed by MRI's own release, not just by the deal database Dealroom.
- **Public housing client counts** of 1,260+ (current page) and 1,600+ (2024 release) come from different dates; report both.
- **ProLease tiers** on the current product page are Enterprise, Central and Direct.
- **Callmax** is an Atlanta multifamily answering service that MRI bought in 2016 ([PRWeb](https://www.prweb.com/releases/mri_software_announces_acquisition_of_callmax/prweb13735128.htm)), not a UK lettings call-handling service.
- **Headcount** is about 5,000, but MRI has never stated an official figure.

## PMX Residential is Geiger's benchmark; Property Tree is its twin

Geiger Property is a US, residential-first platform for "independent landlords, growing property managers and small-to-mid portfolios" ([landing_page.jsx:18-19](../../components/landing/landing_page.jsx)). Its functional breadth is real. It covers:

- leasing, leases, renewals, inspections and deposits
- a double-entry trust ledger with owner statements
- Stripe card and bank (ACH) payments with autopay ([checkout.js](../../lib/payments/checkout.js))
- maintenance with preventive schedules
- resident, owner and vendor portals ([lib/portal/](../../lib/portal))

It is also pre-launch, with no customers ([project note](C:/Users/space/.claude/projects/C--Pro-geiger-property/memory/communications_chat.md)), and it publishes no price. The internal plan targets $1.00 per unit per month with a $25 minimum, falling to $0.75 above 500 units ([COMPETITIVE_FEATURE_ANALYSIS_2026.md:405-411](../../docs/COMPETITIVE_FEATURE_ANALYSIS_2026.md)). That puts Geiger alongside Buildium, AppFolio, DoorLoop and Rentvine, not alongside MRI's enterprise suites.

| MRI product | Overlap with what Geiger does | Same buyer as Geiger? | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| PMX Residential Management + MRI Engage | High: leasing funnel, portals, payments, maintenance, general ledger | Rarely: mid-size and large operators, quote-based pricing, months to implement | The most relevant MRI product and Geiger's upmarket benchmark; it meets Geiger directly only as Geiger's customers grow toward enterprise scale |
| MRI Property Tree and Palace | Highest: small-agency property management, per-property pricing, unlimited users, portals, trust accounting, maintenance, inspections | Same type of buyer, different country (Australia and New Zealand) | The closest product twin and a design and pricing benchmark; a competitor only if Geiger leaves the US |

| Affordable Housing, PHA Pro, ProLease, ManhattanONE, investment suite | None | No | Not competitors |

PMX Residential with Engage is the most relevant MRI product because it does the same job. It is also the comparison buyers and analysts will reach for when Geiger claims "the depth of an enterprise suite". But it rarely meets Geiger's buyer:

- MRI publishes no US price and has no self-serve tier.
- Engage alone takes 60-90 days to implement ([MRI Multifamily CRM](https://www.mrisoftware.com/multifamily/multifamily-crm/)). MRI partners quote 4-9 months for a full rollout ([Assetsoft](https://assetsoft.biz/mri-implementation)), and 6-18 months for mid-sized portfolios ([REdirect Consulting](https://www.redirectconsulting.com/software-solutions/mri)).
- Its US residential review base is small: 27 Capterra reviews for Residential Management ([Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/226831/MRI-Residential-Management/reviews/)).

Unit counts compiled by the integration vendor Propexo show who actually holds US residential units: AppFolio with 9.5 million, RealPage (including Buildium) with 9.0 million and Rent Manager with 5.75 million. MRI's 15.6 million is described as "driven heavily by international units" ([Propexo](https://propexo.com/blog/pms-unit-counts-2026/)). Rentec Direct, sometimes listed under MRI in software directories, has been independently owned since 2007 ([Rentec Direct](https://www.rentecdirect.com/about/)). Geiger's real competitors for its buyer are those firms, not MRI.

Property Tree is the more instructive comparison. At about A$1.30-2.05 per property per month excluding GST, it is priced like a US small-business product. It bundles what Geiger markets, plus things Geiger lacks: AI invoice processing in every tier, 50+ integrations including Xero, and setup "within 48 hours" ([MRI Property Tree pricing](https://www.mrisoftware.com/au/products/property-tree/pricing-1/)). It shows what MRI treats as the minimum for small customers, and in that tier **AI is included, not sold as an extra**. Property Tree is beatable. It scores 3.7 on Capterra from 15 reviews ([Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/211213/MRI-Property-Tree/reviews/)). Users complain about limited reporting ([SoftwareConnect](https://softwareconnect.com/reviews/mri-property-tree/)) and sessions that time out "after a few minutes of staying idle" ([MRI UserVoice](https://mri.uservoice.com/forums/940960-property-tree/suggestions/46836208-property-tree-times-out-to-quickly-having-to-con)). But customers are reluctant to switch: one agency found a rival "better, but not better enough to move away from the last 12 years of use" ([Software Advice](https://www.softwareadvice.com/property/mri-property-tree-profile/reviews/)).

## Geiger can exploit MRI's weak flank, but only after it ships

### MRI is weakest on price, speed, usability and access

**Price** is MRI's most consistent weakness. MRI publishes no US pricing. Its UK G-Cloud list prices put its smallest agency package at **£8,910 a year including six users**, with extra lettings users at £726 a year and professional services at £200 an hour ([G-Cloud 15 pricing](https://assets.applytosupply.digitalmarketplace.service.gov.uk/g-cloud-15/documents/709203/790900596373875-pricing-document-2026-01-28-1713.pdf)). Value for money is the lowest Capterra sub-score on every MRI product that shows sub-scores; for Residential Management it is 3.6 ([Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/226831/MRI-Residential-Management/reviews/)). A competitor's summary of reviews reports complaints about features "locked behind additional module purchases" and "unexpected price increases" ([Facilio, a competitor](https://facilio.com/blog/mri-software-review/)). That UK package costs roughly $11,000 a year. At Geiger's planned $1 per unit per month, the same money would cover about 900 units for a year. The comparison is directional only: the products differ, and Geiger's price is unpublished and assumes banking and AI features that do not exist yet.

**Speed** is the second weakness. Rollouts take 4-18 months, and a migration guide written by a competitor says "every meaningful configuration change typically requires consultant involvement" ([Riooapp, a competitor](https://riooapp.com/blog/migrate-from-mri-software-what-to-expect-map-watch-out-for)). Geiger offers a live demo workspace with no sign-up ([sandbox_client.js](../../lib/sandbox/sandbox_client.js)). But it has no CSV import ([integrations.jsx](../../components/internal/screens/settings/integrations.jsx)), and anyone switching systems needs to bring their data. **Usability** is the third. On G2, MRI scores 7.7-7.8 for ease of use against AppFolio's 9.1, and 7.4 for ease of setup against 8.6 ([G2](https://www.g2.com/compare/appfolio-vs-mri-property-management)). MRI is level with Yardi Voyager, so its usability is normal for enterprise software rather than uniquely poor ([G2](https://g2.com/compare/mri-property-management-vs-yardi-voyager)). Geiger's unified interface is an advantage in principle. It is unproven without users, and AppFolio, not MRI, sets the bar.

**Resident payments and access** are the fourth and fifth weaknesses. MRI's RentPayment iOS app is rated **1.53 stars from 368 ratings** ([AppGoblin](https://appgoblin.info/apps/6446684393)), and the new Engage resident app has too few ratings to show. Geiger includes a resident portal with Stripe card and bank payments and autopay at no extra charge, though it is a web portal, not a native app. MRI's gated, paid API model invites an "actually open" alternative. However, Geiger currently has **no public API at all**, so that advantage stays closed until its security is fixed.

**Support strain** is the sixth weakness. Reviewers describe resolutions that take "weeks or longer" ([Software Advice](https://www.softwareadvice.com/property/mri-software-profile/reviews/)). Reviews from 2025-26 describe work that support used to cover being quoted as paid consultancy ([Facilio, a competitor](https://facilio.com/blog/mri-software-review/)). That comes alongside the May 2026 reorganisation and the pending sale. Customers renewing during an ownership change will look around. Most, though, are larger than Geiger's target buyer.

**Small commercial landlords** are a seventh gap. MRI Commercial Management scores 3.7 on Capterra ([Capterra UK](https://www.capterra.co.uk/reviews/199962/mri-commercial-management)). MRI's UK lease-management add-on starts at £8,000 a year for up to 30 leases ([G-Cloud 15 Evolution pricing](https://assets.applytosupply.digitalmarketplace.service.gov.uk/g-cloud-15/documents/709203/231765895062207-pricing-document-2026-01-29-0843.pdf)). Geiger's shared-cost (CAM) true-up does post to the ledger ([cam_reconciliation.jsx](../../components/internal/screens/accounting/cam_reconciliation.jsx)). But it has no caps, base years, gross-up, inflation (CPI) increases or percentage rent, and the commercial lease terms are saved but never read by any code ([leasing/config.jsx:133-139](../../components/internal/screens/leasing/config.jsx)). The realistic opening is residential managers with a few storefronts in mixed-use buildings, not commercial firms.

AI is **not** a weakness Geiger can use. MRI's AI lacks independent proof, but it is real, it is shipping, and Property Tree bundles it. Geiger has none: its AI Assistant menu item opens a "Coming Soon" screen ([registry.jsx:85-87](../../components/internal/screens/registry.jsx)).

Before any of these openings can be used, Geiger has prerequisites to clear:

- **Security.** Row-level security on every property table is "demo-open" by convention, meaning anyone with the public key can read and write the data ([MIGRATION_CONVENTIONS.md](../../conventions/MIGRATION_CONVENTIONS.md)), and documents sit in a public storage bucket ([property_storage.js](../../lib/supabase/property_storage.js)).
- **Missing providers.** Screening and e-signature are tracking-only ([applications.jsx](../../components/internal/screens/marketing/applications.jsx); [esignature.jsx](../../components/internal/screens/leasing/esignature.jsx)), and SMS is a stub ([deliver.js](../../lib/messaging/deliver.js)).
- **Scheduling.** No scheduled jobs are configured, so rent and fees do not post on their own ([vercel.json](../../vercel.json)).
- **Marketing claims.** The landing page says screening is included ([landing_page.jsx:22-23](../../components/landing/landing_page.jsx)).

MRI's weaknesses are about trust and value, so overclaiming would undercut Geiger's own pitch. The order is: secure the data, connect the missing providers, switch on scheduled jobs, then make the marketing match the code.

### MRI's entrenched lines are no-go zones for Geiger

Seven MRI lines combine entrenched incumbency with a total absence of Geiger capability:

- **Occupier lease accounting.** ProLease has 3,500 clients and the IDC Leader placement. Geiger has no ASC 842 or IFRS 16 support and no tenant-side model.
- **Affordable and public housing.** This needs HUD TRACS submissions, income certifications and the housing-authority voucher work in PHA Pro. Geiger only labels a lease as subsidised and shows the tenant's share ([property_tabs.jsx:229-231](../../components/internal/screens/properties/sections/property_tabs.jsx); [leasing/config.jsx:140-150](../../components/internal/screens/leasing/config.jsx)), so a compliance claim would be false and risky.
- **UK social housing and local government.** MRI is entrenched through the G-Cloud procurement framework and prices mobile working by housing stock ([G-Cloud 15 Mobile Officer pricing](https://assets.applytosupply.digitalmarketplace.service.gov.uk/g-cloud-15/documents/709203/459762816213952-pricing-document-2026-01-30-1234.pdf)).
- **Investment management.** Geiger's own plan rules out "End-to-end real-estate ERP (investment mgmt + fund accounting)" ([COMPETITIVE_FEATURE_ANALYSIS_2026.md:377](../../docs/COMPETITIVE_FEATURE_ANALYSIS_2026.md)).
- **Workplace and facilities management.** The buyer is a facilities or workplace manager, not a property manager.
- **Enterprise commercial and retail.** This means recoveries depth, percentage rent and multi-currency, plus Simplicity in Asia.
- **The Australian and New Zealand agency market.** Property Tree and Palace now bundle the Proptech Labs tools, and Geiger handles only US dollars and US tax.

The largest multifamily operators belong on the same list. MRI's NMHC presence comes through attached products that Geiger cannot match, and those buyers expect the integrations and security reviews Geiger cannot yet pass. Homeowner associations should stay off the roadmap unless Geiger deliberately chooses them; there it would face Jenark and CondoPlus.

## Conclusion

MRI's shape matters more to Geiger than any single MRI feature. MRI enters small-customer markets by buying the local leader and keeping it on its own engine, as it did with Property Tree, Palace, Qube and the UK council systems. Agora then sits over the result. The US small-landlord market has no such MRI asset. So the realistic way MRI would ever meet Geiger head-on is an acquisition of a US small-business vendor, perhaps by a new owner after the exit, not an in-house product. That is an inference; nothing in 2024-2026 news points to it, but it is the trigger to watch. Property Tree also changes what "differentiation" means for Geiger. MRI's own small-customer product already bundles AI, charges per property, offers unlimited users and promises a 48-hour setup. So "AI and portals included" is the minimum for the segment, not a moat.

Geiger's real exposure to MRI comes when its customers outgrow it. A manager who outgrows a small-business tool will evaluate PMX Residential, Yardi Voyager and Entrata. The capabilities that keep those customers are the ones Geiger's own plan reserves for its Scale tier: separate books for multiple ownership entities, an open API and deeper reporting. MRI's pending sale and support strain create a switching window. But that window rewards vendors that are live, secure and able to migrate a customer's data, so for Geiger the binding constraint is its own readiness, not MRI.
