# MRI Software: platform extensibility (Agora, APIs, low-code, integrations) and AI lease abstraction. Fact check (as of 2026-11-02)

Scope: six claims from a pasted text comparing "Geiger" with MRI. Verdict scale: TRUE / PARTLY TRUE / OVERSTATED / FALSE / UNVERIFIABLE. Most MRI capability material is vendor marketing; independent sources are noted where available.

Product-name notes, which apply to every claim:
- "MRI X" is not an MRI product name. The closest real names are **Property Management X (PMX)**, MRI's next-generation property management system announced in May 2024, and the older **MRI Platform X®** ERP brand, which still appears in partner documentation such as Procore's legacy ERP archive. Agora is not a component of PMX. It is a cross-product platform that sits over PMX, Horizon, Qube PM and other MRI apps.
- "Leverton" (acquired 2019) is now **MRI Contract Intelligence**. An undated MRI webinar also uses the name "MRI Lease Intelligence" alongside PMX.
- **Agora Insights** (2022) is the BI/data product. **Ask Agora** (Oct 2025) is the AI companion. **Agora Intelligence** and **Agora Orchestrator** (June 2026) are the newest Agora products.

## Claim 1: "MRI X's crowning achievement is its Agora framework, an architecture built to be heavily modified." What is MRI Agora?

### Takeaway
Verdict: **OVERSTATED (mischaracterized).** MRI Agora exists and is central to MRI's strategy. MRI announced it in October 2022 as an "open and connected" real-estate platform: a shared data layer (Agora Insights), shared services and a unified UX across MRI and partner apps. From 2025 to 2026 it became mainly an **AI platform**: Ask Agora (Oct 2025), then Agora Intelligence and Agora Orchestrator (June 2026). No MRI or analyst source calls it a developer framework or an architecture "built to be heavily modified." It is also not a feature of "MRI X" or PMX. It spans MRI's product line.

### Cited Findings
- **Launch, Oct 25, 2022 (Ascend, New Orleans, Oct 23-26):** MRI called Agora its "next generation," "open and connected technology platform." Saurabh Abhyankar said: "Developed with an AI-first approach, the MRI Agora platform brings a brand-new real estate data platform." Launch products were MRI Agora Insights, AI-driven MRI Forms Intelligence and MRI Chat. Agora Insights "allows for advanced analytics across MRI and non-MRI data sources." The release does not describe Agora as a developer framework or a low-code/customization tool. — [MRI press release via PR Newswire, 2022-10-25](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/mri-software-unveils-its-next-generation-real-estate-platform-301658268.html); [MRI newsroom copy](https://www.mrisoftware.com/news/mri-software-unveils-its-next-generation-real-estate-platform/)
- **Independent analyst view (Verdantix, Dec 9, 2022):** Agora "leverag[es] the Microsoft Azure platform" and "creates a data lake that replicates the target data in different client systems." It adds "a layer of shared services and APIs to deliver single sign on, payments, chatbots, e-signatures and AI tools" and "a customisable common environment with a consistent web and mobile UX to access all MRI and partner solutions." Verdantix called the plan "ambitious," said customers would benefit "once MRI delivers this fully," and said it "is not going to happen overnight." It also noted that MRI's earlier acquisitions had produced little synergy, so multi-app customers relied on manual transfers and Excel. — [Verdantix blog, 2022-12-09](https://www.verdantix.com/insights/blog/where-ambition-meets-innovation-mri-software-unveils-new-agora-platform)
- **Oct 20, 2025 (Ascend, Nashville):** MRI unveiled **Ask Agora**, "its AI companion that transforms data and actions into intelligent workflows," using "agentic AI to leverage data from multiple sources." MRI said the Agora platform "connects MRI products, partner solutions, and third-party applications into a unified experience," with a core that is "a secure infrastructure that aggregates and harmonizes data from all relevant sources" and the backbone of Agora Insights. — [MRI press release via PR Newswire, 2025-10-20](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/mri-software-unveils-advanced-ai-companion-to-transform-real-estate-workflows-302588719.html)
- **June 11, 2026 (Ascend EMEA, London):** A UK MRI consultancy wrote that "Agora is no longer simply a connectivity platform." It is now "the underlying architecture that enables AI-powered workflows, data integration, and application interoperability" across MRI apps including PMX, Horizon and Qube PM. — [REdirect Consulting, 2026-07-01](https://www.redirectconsulting.com/blog/mri-emea-ascend-2026-how-agora-ai-and-agentic-automation-are-transforming-property-management). Agora then reached APAC at Ascend APAC 2026 (title-level only; not fetched). — [REdirect Consulting, APAC 2026](https://www.redirectconsulting.com/blog/mri-ascend-apac-2026-mri-agora-ai-agentic-automation-and-the-future-of-pmx)
- **June 25, 2026:** MRI launched **Agora Intelligence**, which sends daily role-specific briefings and classifies findings as risks, trends, watches or opportunities with confidence and data-completeness scores. It also launched **Agora Orchestrator**, which runs "real estate-specific agentic workflows automatically, with the governance and audit trail enterprise operators require" from a **pre-built agent catalog**. MRI describes Agora as combining "intelligence, workflow execution, and governance in a single platform," "Built on a shared real estate data foundation." Both products are available globally to MRI clients. — [MRI press release, 2026-06-25](https://www.mrisoftware.com/news/mri-software-launches-agora-intelligence-and-agora-orchestrator-delivering-ai-powered-recommendations-and-workflow-execution-for-real-estate/)
- Commercial Observer reported that Agora Intelligence is an expansion of the Agora Insights dashboarding product, led by new Chief Innovation Officer Nihar Malik. — [Commercial Observer, 2026-06-25](https://commercialobserver.com/2026/06/mri-software-data-platform-ai-expansion/)
- **Oct 6, 2026:** London block manager JAR adopted MRI Agora Orchestrator for agentic workflows "within agreed business rules and guardrails." This was a customer win, not a product launch. — [MRI news](https://www.mrisoftware.com/news/jar-adopts-mri-softwares-agentic-ai-to-streamline-workflows-and-support-resident-service/)
- **PMX announced May 2024** as an AI-enabled, rebuilt next-generation property management system. — [MRI AU news](https://www.mrisoftware.com/au/news/revolutionising-real-estate-efficiency-next-generation-property-management-system/)

### Inferences
- Agora has been, in order: (a) a connectivity and data platform with a BI product (2022); (b) an AI companion layer (2025); (c) an agentic AI and governance platform (2026). Of the categories the brief asks about, it is a BI product (Agora Insights), a data platform and an AI platform. It is not a developer framework.
- "Built to be heavily modified" inverts MRI's message. MRI's 2026 pitch is that clients do *not* need to build their own semantic layers, validation rules or governance. Orchestrator ships a pre-built agent catalog.
- Saying "MRI X's crowning achievement" mixes up product names. Agora is the umbrella platform, and PMX is one of the apps under it.

### Gaps
- I found no public technical documentation of Agora's extension points, such as whether clients or partners can author their own Orchestrator agents.
- I found no official MRI announcement list for the main MRI Ascend 2026 in Las Vegas. Agora news from Oct-Nov 2026, if any, is not covered.

## Claim 2: "MRI allows corporate IT teams to build completely customized database tables, screens, and workflows" (low-code/no-code)

### Takeaway
Verdict: **OVERSTATED; the "completely customized screens" part is unsupported.** MRI's public extensibility story is about integration and configuration: the MIX integration toolkit, gated APIs, the Partner Connect program, a configurable portal UX, and pre-built AI agents. I found no MRI low-code or no-code app builder that lets client IT create custom tables, screens and workflows. Third parties describe MRI API access as gated, with no self-service developer portal and no general sandbox.

### Cited Findings
- **MRI Information Exchange (MIX), Apr 22, 2015:** MIX is "a powerful enabler of custom software connections," a "toolkit for interface development" for "more complex and tailored integrations" with "the third-party solution of the client's choice." MRI stated its goal "to have the most flexible, configurable software on the market." At that time Partner Connect had "over 30 companies." — [MRI news, 2015-04-22](https://www.mrisoftware.com/nz/?p=23251)
- **API access model (third-party integration guide):** Access requires an agreement through Partner Connect (Solution Partner category) or an introduction sponsored by an MRI client. An NDA may be required before documentation is shared, and MIX API licensing may carry a fee. Calls use HTTP Basic auth with a MIX Partner Key. Partner keys can call only APIs registered in MRI's MIX manifest. "There is no self-service developer portal for this key." "MRI does not offer a general-purpose sandbox to middleware and integration providers." — [Apideck MRI configuration guide](https://developers.apideck.com/apis/accounting/mrisoftware/configuration)
- Supergood's API Report Card grades MRI **F**. It says MRI publishes API design guides, but access is gated behind Partner Connect or enterprise agreements, and custom API work often goes to third-party consultants. Supergood sells an unofficial MRI API, so it has an interest in the grade. — [Supergood](https://supergood.ai/api-report-card/mri-software)
- **Partner Connect** is MRI's partner program: an "open and connected ecosystem [that] empowers clients to choose the best solutions." The page gives no partner counts and no marketplace details. — [MRI Partners](https://partners.mrisoftware.com/English/). An MRI-affiliated site (Rockend) cites 140+ Partner Connect partners and an MRI Application eXchange (MAX) with 175+ apps via the client portal (search snippet only; not fetched). — [Rockend](https://rockend.com.au)
- Verdantix (2022) describes Agora as offering "a customisable common environment with a consistent web and mobile UX." This means a configurable portal over MRI and partner apps, not a table or screen builder. — [Verdantix](https://www.verdantix.com/insights/blog/where-ambition-meets-innovation-mri-software-unveils-new-agora-platform)
- Agora Orchestrator (2026) runs workflows from a **pre-built agent catalog**, and MRI pitches that clients need not build their own semantic layers or governance. — [MRI press release, 2026-06-25](https://www.mrisoftware.com/news/mri-software-launches-agora-intelligence-and-agora-orchestrator-delivering-ai-powered-recommendations-and-workflow-execution-for-real-estate/)
- The UK G-Cloud listing for an MRI commercial portal states no API documentation and no sandbox, and a GetApp listing says MRI Property Management has no API. These may describe older or different products. — [G-Cloud listing](https://www.applytosupply.digitalmarketplace.service.gov.uk/g-cloud/services/824185036996613); [GetApp](https://www.getapp.com/real-estate-property-software/a/mri-software)

### Inferences
- MRI is configurable and integrable through partner-gated APIs, MIX and the marketplace. The claim describes a low-code platform in the style of Salesforce or ServiceNow, which I found no evidence for.
- Configuring an enterprise ERP like MRI usually involves user-defined fields, custom reports and implementation-partner work. That is far from "completely customized … screens."

### Gaps
- I could not verify from public docs whether MRI Commercial Management, Residential or PMX support user-defined fields or tables, report designers or workflow designers. A Platform X UDF search returned only unrelated products, and MRI's admin guides appear to sit behind myMRI login. Treat UDF-level customization as plausible but unverified.
- I found no current official partner or marketplace counts on an MRI-owned page. The 2015 figure was 30+ partners, and the MIX page footer shows "400+ Partners" as a company statistic.

## Claim 3: "native, deep-data pipelines into corporate tools like Salesforce, Yardi, or complex third-party BI warehouses"

### Takeaway
Verdict: **OVERSTATED, and the Yardi part is likely FALSE.** I found no native MRI-built Salesforce connector. MRI-Salesforce integration goes through consultants, iPaaS (Exelement SyncCloud) or custom builds. Yardi is MRI's direct competitor. The only official MRI-Yardi link found is a narrow integration of MRI's VaultWare apartment reservation system, and MRI-to-Yardi moves are custom ETL migrations. For BI, Agora Insights centralizes MRI and non-MRI data in an Azure data lake. I found no evidence of a native Snowflake or Fabric data share. Third-party connectors exist (Informatica, read-only), and Power BI users connect through SQL.

## Claim 4: AI lease abstraction that ingests a 100-page commercial lease, identifies obligations, clauses and dates, and "automatically build[s] the tenant record in the system"

### Takeaway
Verdict: **PARTLY TRUE.** MRI has offered AI lease abstraction since buying LEVERTON in 2019, now sold as **MRI Contract Intelligence**. It extracts hundreds of standardized fields with links back to the source text. MRI's own materials describe a **human-reviewed** process: low-confidence values go to reviewers, "multiple layers of expert review" are applied, and only validated data is then loaded into lease administration and accounting. It is a **separate product** that integrates with MRI's lease admin systems and PMX. It is not a native PMX feature that builds tenant records hands-free. No MRI announcement found applies Agora AI or agents to lease abstraction.

### Cited Findings
- **Acquisition:** MRI acquired AI data-extraction company LEVERTON (reported date July 31, 2019). LEVERTON offered its tech as an engine through APIs and "AI as a Service." It became an MRI business unit, rebranded MRI Contract Intelligence. — [MRI acquisition release](https://www.mrisoftware.com/uk/?p=25062); [The Negotiator](https://thenegotiator.co.uk/news/mri-software-acquires-ai-poineer-leverton/)
- **Current product page:** "uses AI-powered lease abstraction to digitize, structure and connect critical contract data." Abstraction is "supported by multiple layers of expert review to improve accuracy." "Abstracted data flows directly into a single source of truth" connected to MRI's lease administration and accounting platform. It covers "hundreds of standardized fields" and 20-25+ languages. Vendor metrics: 2M+ documents, 200+ organizations, 75% less manual data entry, 80% fewer errors, 50-75% faster lease review. Managed lease administration services are offered separately. — [MRI Contract Intelligence](https://www.mrisoftware.com/products/contract-intelligence/)
- **MRI's own process description (Oct 15, 2026):** AI makes a first pass and records page and paragraph for each value. "Values the model is unsure about are routed to reviewers instead of being accepted automatically." Amendment conflicts are "escalated for expert review." Reviewers fully check high-impact fields, sample lower-risk ones and tie rent to payments and the GL. Only then does "validated data" load into lease management software. MRI names ASC 842 inputs, handwritten changes, poor scans and ambiguous amendments as needing human review. The post mentions LLM clause summaries and warns of confabulation. — [MRI blog, 2026-10-15](https://www.mrisoftware.com/blog/what-is-the-contract-abstraction-process/)
- **PMX link:** An undated MRI on-demand webinar, "MRI Lease Intelligence and PMX," promises to "extract, analyze, and consume accurate data directly in your property management system," with "a direct link to the original lease agreement." It does not describe automatic record creation. — [MRI webinar](https://www.mrisoftware.com/resources/mri-lease-intelligence-and-pmx-effective-lease-management-with-accurate-data-at-your-fingertips/)
- Savills expanded its use of the tool to include integration with MRI ProLease. — [Savills News](https://www.savills.co.uk/insight-and-opinion/savills-news/312786/savills--mri-software-announce-expanded-partnership)
- The June 2026 Agora Intelligence/Orchestrator release mentions no document or lease-abstraction agents. Agora Intelligence appears on the Contract Intelligence page only as a companion that turns contract data into portfolio insights. — [MRI, 2026-06-25](https://www.mrisoftware.com/news/mri-software-launches-agora-intelligence-and-agora-orchestrator-delivering-ai-powered-recommendations-and-workflow-execution-for-real-estate/); [MRI Contract Intelligence](https://www.mrisoftware.com/products/contract-intelligence/)
- MRI says it was named a Leader in the 2026 IDC MarketScape for Worldwide AI-Enabled Lease Administration Applications (title-level; vendor-reported). — [MRI news](https://www.mrisoftware.com/news/mri-software-named-a-leader-in-2026-idc-marketscape-for-worldwide-ai-enabled-lease-administration-applications/)

### Inferences
- The capability in the claim (ingesting long leases, extracting obligations, clauses and dates) is real and mature. The overstatement is in "automatically build the tenant record." MRI itself describes a human-in-the-loop flow, and its marketing presents the human review as a selling point.
- MRI lists "Leverton" as an outdated name. Treat "Lease Intelligence" (in the webinar) as an alternate or older label for the same capability.

### Gaps
- No public documentation shows the exact PMX import mechanics: whether validated abstracts create or update lease records automatically, or through a staged import.
- The "100-page" figure is unverifiable but plausible. No source states a page limit.
- Whether Contract Intelligence is licensed separately from PMX is not stated publicly. It is marketed as its own product.

## Claim 5: "MRI utilizes advanced AI directly within the database layer"

### Takeaway
Verdict: **OVERSTATED or unsupported.** MRI describes AI running *on top of* a harmonized data foundation: an Azure data lake (2022), "secure infrastructure that aggregates and harmonizes data" (2025), and a "shared real estate data foundation" and "semantic layer" (2026). No source says AI runs inside the database. The phrasing looks like a garbled version of MRI's "data foundation and semantic layer" messaging.

### Cited Findings
- Agora "creates a data lake that replicates the target data in different client systems" on Microsoft Azure. — [Verdantix, 2022-12-09](https://www.verdantix.com/insights/blog/where-ambition-meets-innovation-mri-software-unveils-new-agora-platform)
- MRI's core is "a secure infrastructure that aggregates and harmonizes data from all relevant sources," the backbone for Agora Insights and Ask Agora. — [PR Newswire, 2025-10-20](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/mri-software-unveils-advanced-ai-companion-to-transform-real-estate-workflows-302588719.html)
- Agora is "Built on a shared real estate data foundation." MRI uses "the most appropriate model for each workload" with a consistent governance and orchestration layer on top. — [MRI, 2026-06-25](https://www.mrisoftware.com/news/mri-software-launches-agora-intelligence-and-agora-orchestrator-delivering-ai-powered-recommendations-and-workflow-execution-for-real-estate/)
- Malik described "a translation layer, or what it is called a semantic layer," that lets AI models work with MRI's data. — [Commercial Observer, 2026-06-25](https://commercialobserver.com/2026/06/mri-software-data-platform-ai-expansion/)

### Inferences
- MRI's architecture as described is a centralized data platform, a semantic layer, then multiple models plus an orchestration and governance layer. That is AI *over* data, not AI *in* the database layer.

### Gaps
- MRI publishes no architecture detail on where model inference runs or which database or AI services it uses beyond Azure (2022).
