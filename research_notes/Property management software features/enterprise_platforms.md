# Enterprise / Mid-Market Residential PM Platforms: Feature Catalogs (AppFolio, Yardi, Entrata, RealPage, ResMan)

Research date: 2026-10-16. Scope: AppFolio Property Manager (Core/Plus/Max), Yardi (Breeze, Breeze Premier, Voyager + RentCafe), Entrata, RealPage, ResMan. Sources are mostly vendor pages and press releases, so the performance figures here are vendor claims. Note: yardi.com (US) returned HTTP 403 to every fetch this session. Yardi Voyager/RentCafe detail therefore comes from search snippets, regional Yardi pages and yardibreeze.com, and is thinner than the other platforms.

## Q1. What does each platform list as features per module, and which are tier-gated or paid add-ons?

### Takeaway
Only AppFolio, Yardi Breeze and ResMan publish tiered packaging. AppFolio has Core $1.49, Plus $3.20 and Max $5.00 per unit per month, with a 50-unit minimum. Breeze is $1/unit with a $100 minimum, and Breeze Premier is $1/unit with a $400 minimum. ResMan's tiers are Start, Scale and Thrive, with no published prices. Entrata, RealPage and Yardi Voyager sell à-la-carte product catalogs with quote-based pricing. Across all five, advanced capabilities are tier-gated or sold as add-ons: CRM, API write access, budgeting, custom reports, affordable/student compliance, websites, AI agents, utility billing and AP automation.

### Inferences
**Cross-platform baseline (all five):**
- Properties/units setup; online application and screening; e-lease; resident portal with online payments (ACH/card)
- Maintenance work orders; GL/AP/AR accounting; budgeting (often gated); standard reporting
- Renters insurance; ILS syndication; CRM/lead management; AI leasing agent

**Common tier-gate pattern to copy:**
- Basic tier: accounting, leasing, maintenance, portals
- Mid tier: budgeting, custom reports/fields, approval workflows, API read access, specialty verticals
- Top tier: CRM, dynamic pricing, read/write API, websites, chatbot, dedicated CSM

**Common add-ons:**
- Screening upgrades (income verification, fraud detection)
- Utility billing / RUBS
- AP/invoice OCR outsourcing
- Websites, contact center
- Deposit alternatives
- Insurance programs: renters, master policy, liability-to-landlord

**Trust accounting/owner statements:** owner and fractional-ownership accounting with reserves is explicit only for Yardi Breeze and AppFolio (Owner Portal). The enterprise multifamily vendors (Entrata, RealPage, ResMan) do not emphasize third-party owner trust accounting in the material reviewed. This matches their institutional-multifamily focus.

## Q2. What property types/verticals does each platform serve?

### Takeaway
- **Broadest vertical coverage:** Yardi (residential, commercial, affordable, self-storage, associations and manufactured housing even on Breeze Premier, plus Voyager suites) and Entrata (multifamily, student, affordable, military, commercial, manufactured housing).
- **AppFolio:** SFR, multifamily, student, affordable, HOA, commercial and investment management.
- **RealPage:** conventional, affordable, senior, student, military and commercial markets.
- **ResMan:** the narrowest focus, conventional multifamily and affordable only.

### Cited Findings
- **AppFolio:**
  - Verticals: single family, multifamily, student housing, affordable housing, community associations (HOA), commercial, investment management — [AppFolio Property Manager](https://www.appfolio.com/property-manager)

### Inferences
- A Geiger Property MVP aimed at SMB-to-mid-market should cover conventional residential (SFR plus multifamily) first. Affordable is the most common premium vertical: it is gated or premium-priced at AppFolio, Breeze and ResMan.
- Student, military and senior are enterprise-only differentiators (Entrata, RealPage, Yardi Voyager).
- Associations (HOA) appear at AppFolio and at Yardi Breeze Premier. Self-storage and manufactured housing appear at Yardi and Entrata (MH).

### Gaps
- Senior living vertical depth was not verified (Yardi Senior Living Suite; RealPage senior market).
- Commercial feature depth (CAM reconciliation, lease abstraction) was not verified for AppFolio, Entrata or Breeze.
