# SMB Property Management Platforms: Feature Catalog (Buildium, Rent Manager, Propertyware, DoorLoop, Rentvine, Yardi Breeze)

Research date: 2026-10-16. Sources are mostly official pricing/feature pages and help centers, fetched live. Where an official page could not be fetched (Rent Manager /features and Propertyware /features both returned HTTP 404), third-party sources were used and flagged.

## Q1. What does each platform list per module, and what is tier-gated vs add-on? (includes pricing tiers)

### Takeaway
All six cover the same core: properties/units, listings, applications and screening, e-sign leasing, tenant/owner portals, trust accounting, payments, maintenance and reporting. They differ in packaging. Buildium gates heavily by tier (fees, automations, API, AI Workforce). Rent Manager gates by bundle (mobile app, mapping, video inspections, AI). DoorLoop restructured into One/Connect/Multiply, with AI agents sold as add-ons. Propertyware lists the same 17 features across tiers and sells call centers, websites and insurance as add-ons. Rentvine quotes per unit and includes AI (Fixie, MCP). Yardi Breeze splits Breeze vs Breeze Premier, and Premier adds approvals, job costing, CRM queue and property websites.

### Inferences
- A Geiger Property MVP feature set is the intersection of all six: properties/units/owners, listings syndication, applications and screening, e-sign leases, trust accounting with owner statements and distributions, ePay, work orders and vendors, three portals (tenant/owner/vendor), and reports with export.

## Q3. What automation/workflow builders exist?

### Takeaway
DoorLoop has the most clearly documented no-code builder: Workflows built from triggers, actions, rules and monitoring, with templates. Buildium sells "Workflow Automations" plus rule-based automations and its AI Workforce. Rent Manager lists "Custom Workflows" in every bundle, but public detail is scarce. Rentvine relies on pipeline workflows (renewals), AI agents and an open API/MCP. Yardi Breeze Premier offers AP approval workflows and move-in/move-out workflows.

### Cited Findings
- **DoorLoop Workflows:**
  - Triggers ("what starts the workflow", e.g. lease dates, move-ins). Actions (emails, tasks, messages, field updates). Rules (delays, timing, conditions). Monitoring (activity, completed steps, errors).
  - Pre-built templates run as-is, edited, or built from scratch. Edits apply to future runs immediately. No tier gating stated.
  - Sources: [DoorLoop Workflows](https://doorloop.com/features/workflows); [Getting started with workflows](https://support.doorloop.com/en/articles/12903573-getting-started-with-workflows)
- **DoorLoop Zapier:** polling triggers (property, portfolio, owner, status, payment method). Older Premium tier listed the Zapier integration and API — [DoorLoop search summary](https://support.doorloop.com/en/articles/12903573-getting-started-with-workflows); [usecarly](https://www.usecarly.com/blog/doorloop-pricing/)
- **Buildium automation rows:** Resident Payment Reminders; Automated Tenant Screening; Returned Payment Automation; Preferred Approvers Automation (bill approvals); Bank Reconciliation Rules; Reporting Automation; Workflow Automations; Unit Turn Automation. The full Automations suite is on Premium — [Buildium Pricing](https://www.buildium.com/pricing/)
- **Buildium AI controls:** rules for when the AI acts, assists or steps back — [Lumina AI Workforce](https://www.buildium.com/features/lumina-ai-workforce/)
- **Rent Manager:** "Custom Workflows" in all bundles; "Report Automation" (Report Batches); AI-Powered Script Builder on Premium. No official trigger/action documentation found. A Rent Manager webinar promotes external automation via Zapier, Microsoft Power Automate and Integromat. Consultancy IT In Flow sells workflow and scripting services — [Rent Manager Pricing](https://www.rentmanager.com/pricing/); [Tech Tuesday](https://www.rentmanager.com/?p=42165); [IT In Flow](https://www.rentmanager.com/?p=60740)
- **Rentvine:**
