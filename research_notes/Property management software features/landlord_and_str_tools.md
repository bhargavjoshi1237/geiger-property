# Independent-Landlord / DIY Rental Tools and Short-Term-Rental (STR) Platforms: Feature Inventory (2025–2026)

Scope: TenantCloud, TurboTenant, Avail (Realtor.com), RentRedi, Hemlane, Innago, Apartments.com Rental Tools, Zillow Rental Manager; STR: Guesty, Hostaway, OwnerRez, Hospitable. Research date: October 2026. Pricing pages were fetched live where possible; Zillow, Apartments.com and Innago pages blocked or gave thin content, so those rows lean on secondary sources (flagged).

## What does each product offer per module, and what is free vs paid?

### Takeaway
DIY landlord tools follow a pattern: the landlord gets the core software free or cheap (Avail, Innago, Zillow and Apartments.com charge nothing; TurboTenant and Hemlane have free tiers), and the money comes from fees renters pay. Those are screening at $30–$55, ACH at $0–$2.50, card payments at 2.75–3.5%, opt-in rent reporting at $4.99–$5.99/mo, and partner renters insurance. Paid tiers ($9–$60/mo, or per-unit) unlock state-specific/custom leases, free ACH, faster payouts, income verification, bank sync and accounting, and owner portals. STR platforms charge per listing or property, with modular add-ons for AI, dynamic pricing, payments, websites, owner accounting, damage protection and smart locks.

### Inferences
- Features a full-suite PM product (AppFolio/Buildium-class) may lack, or execute less simply, than these DIY tools:
  - Self-serve, state-specific, attorney-reviewed lease builders with auto-inserted state disclosures and clauses (Avail LocalAssist, TurboTenant, Hemlane, Zillow).
  - An AI lease compliance audit for uploaded third-party leases (TurboTenant).
  - Opt-in rent reporting to credit bureaus paid by the renter (TurboTenant, RentRedi, Avail CreditBoost).
  - Cash rent at retail stores (RentRedi).
  - One-click syndication to 19–25+ sites included free.
  - Flat pricing with unlimited units, and a free tier.
  - Renter-paid screening with landlord opt-in to absorb the fee.
  - Optional on-demand services alongside self-management: placement, inspections, eviction prevention, 24/7 maintenance coordination (Hemlane, TurboTenant Autopilot).
  - Forms packs and notices.
  - Free landlord-side tax outputs (Schedule E, Form 8825).
- The monetization pattern worth copying is "free software + renter-paid convenience fees + premium tier that waives renter ACH fees and speeds payouts".
- Gated tiers usually unlock lease customization, income verification, multiple bank accounts, team roles, and owner portals.

## Which STR features are relevant for a long-term residential PM product to replicate (e.g. furnished/mid-term rentals)?

### Takeaway
Mid-term (30+ day furnished) demand is growing beyond travel nurses, and STR platforms are adding mid-term channels. The STR capabilities that carry over best to a long-term or mid-term product are:
- Multi-channel availability and rate sync (e.g. Furnished Finder).
- A unified multi-channel inbox with AI replies and message-to-task conversion.
- Turnover and cleaning task automation tied to move-out.
- Guest/tenant ID verification.
- Deposit-free damage protection.
- Smart-lock code automation.
- A direct-booking or branded listing site with online payment.
- Owner statements and trust accounting.

### Inferences
- For a long-term residential PM product, the highest-value STR imports appear to be:
  - Smart-lock access codes generated per lease or showing, and revoked at move-out, which also enables self-guided showings.
  - Move-out-triggered turnover task chains (clean, inspect, repair) with vendor payouts.
  - A unified inbox covering SMS, email and portal, with AI drafts and message-to-work-order conversion.
  - ID/identity verification and deposit alternatives (damage-protection or surety in place of a cash deposit).
  - Furnished-unit fields: inventory and amenities, utilities-included pricing.
  - Calendar-based availability with variable stay lengths and per-month or per-night pricing for furnished/mid-term units.
  - Syndication to mid-term channels (Furnished Finder, Airbnb monthly).
  - Upsells (early move-in, parking, cleaning) through a tenant portal.
- Lower relevance for pure long-term: nightly dynamic pricing, OTA channel managers (Airbnb/Booking.com), and review-response tools. Dynamic pricing logic could still inform rent-price recommendations at renewal or vacancy.

- Listing description writers: TurboTenant, TenantCloud.

- AI lease-reading onboarding and AI accounting: RentRedi, late 2025.
- An AI leasing assistant: Zillow with EliseAI, 2025.

### Inferences
- The leading-edge AI pattern for 2026 is an agentic inbox: auto-reply with confidence thresholds and escalation, message-to-task conversion, issue/complaint detection, and natural-language querying of portfolio data. Exposing data through an MCP server (Hospitable) is a new differentiator.
- Several of these patterns map directly onto long-term PM: tenant message triage into work orders, lease extraction at onboarding, receipt OCR for accounting, AI lease compliance checks, and AI listing copy.

### Gaps
- No launch dates were found for TurboTenant's Description Generator, TenantCloud's Cloudia, or OwnerRez's Rezzy AI.
- No AI features were found for Avail, Hemlane, Innago or Apartments.com. This is absence of evidence from the pages checked, not proof of absence.
- Dates for some Guesty 2026 items (Agents Hub, complaint detection) come from secondary summaries and are not fully confirmed.
