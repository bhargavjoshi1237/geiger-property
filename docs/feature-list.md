# Geiger Property — Feature List

Source: distilled from [`competitive-feature-matrix.md`](./competitive-feature-matrix.md)
(research date 2026-07-03) and scored in [`feature-selection.md`](./feature-selection.md).

Positioning: *"Enterprise-suite depth for independent landlords and growing
property managers — every table-stakes feature included, the cheap differentiators
free, and AI + fintech the big platforms lock behind $1,500/mo minimums."*

This is the flat enumeration of the product surface. Each parent area maps to a
top-level sidebar item in `components/internal/sidebar/sidebar_nav.jsx`; each leaf
maps to a sub-item. `[P0]`–`[P4]` = relevancy (see feature-selection.md).

- **Overview** `[P0]` — portfolio dashboard (occupancy, rent, delinquency, tasks).
- **Properties** — all properties, units, portfolios, buildings & blocks, amenities,
  photos & media, floor plans, unit types, ownership & splits, unit turns &
  make-ready, keys & access, property groups.
- **Listings & Marketing** — vacancy listings, listing syndication, marketing
  website, ILS distribution, AI listing writer, virtual tours, rent comparables,
  showings & tours, self-guided showings, waitlist, promotions, QR & yard signs.
- **Leads & CRM** — prospects, pipeline, guest cards, follow-ups, lead sources,
  lead-to-lease, auto-responders.
- **Applications & Screening** — online applications, application forms, tenant
  screening, credit/background/eviction checks, income verification, application
  fees, decisioning, applicant communication.
- **Leasing** — leases, lease builder, templates, state-specific leases, e-signature,
  renewals, rent increases, move-in/out, move-in inspection, notices & violations,
  addenda, security deposits, deposit alternatives, lease expirations.
- **Tenants** — directory, resident portal, profiles, household & occupants,
  communication log, announcements, documents, renters insurance, rent reporting,
  notices, renter rewards, reviews & feedback, move-out pipeline.
- **Owners** — directory, owner portal, statements, distributions, contributions,
  ownership splits, management fees, 1099 & tax, communication, documents.
- **Maintenance** — work orders, requests, AI maintenance triage, vendors,
  assignments, vendor portal, 24/7 coordination, preventive maintenance, recurring
  work orders, inspections, make-ready boards, inventory & parts, estimates & bids,
  mobile maintenance, photos, meter readings.
- **Accounting** — rent collection, online payments, autopay, recurring charges,
  late fees, general ledger, chart of accounts, bills & payables, invoices &
  receivables, bank accounts, bank reconciliation, trust accounting, AI bookkeeping,
  journal entries, deposits, payouts & distributions, budgeting, 1099 e-filing,
  QuickBooks sync, utility billing (RUBS), payment plans, cash/retail payments.
- **Banking & Fintech** — landlord banking, property sub-accounts, high-yield
  savings, debit & cards, payment methods, merchant/payout accounts, flexible rent,
  refunds, loan marketplace, transaction history.
- **Communications** — inbox, email, text/SMS, bulk messaging, announcements,
  templates, call logging, VoIP & voicemail, notifications, contact center.
- **Documents & eSign** — document library, templates, e-signature, shared files,
  compliance documents, document requests.
- **Tasks & Operations** — tasks, calendar, reminders, automations, AI assistant,
  recurring tasks, team assignments.
- **Associations & HOA** `[P4]` — associations, board members, dues & assessments,
  violations, architectural requests, committees, voting & elections, meetings &
  minutes, common areas, amenity booking.
- **Reports** — financial reports, rent roll, delinquency, occupancy, income
  statement, balance sheet, cash flow, general ledger, owner statements, Schedule E /
  tax pack, maintenance & leasing reports, custom report builder, scheduled reports,
  export center.
- **Insights** — portfolio KPIs, occupancy trends, revenue & NOI, delinquency trends,
  lease expiration forecast, rent optimization, AI insights, benchmarks.
- **Portals** — resident, owner, vendor, board, applicant portals, portal branding,
  mobile app.
- **Integrations** — accounting sync, payment gateways, screening providers, listing
  partners, insurance partners, Zapier, open API, webhooks, data import, connected apps.
- **Settings** — company profile, team & members, roles & permissions, billing &
  plans, branding, white-label, custom fields, notifications, security, SSO, 2FA,
  audit logs, tax & legal, localization, data & backup, API & webhooks, support & SLA.
