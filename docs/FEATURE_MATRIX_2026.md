## How to read this matrix for Geiger Property

- **●-dense rows in Universals + Common** = our P0/P1 (ship these).
- **★ rows in Uncommon** = where we steal positioning: banking (Baselane-style), AI (AppFolio-style), workflow (Rentvine-style) — bundle **free**.
- **Rare + Extremely rare rows** = P4 (skip). Enterprise moats, not our buyer.

Full strategy + P0–P4 selection rationale in `COMPETITIVE_FEATURE_ANALYSIS_2026.md`.
